一、适用范围与基本原则

本说明适用于老干部活动中心制作、由本政策载明的运营主体提供的《佛系种地》Android 原生合规版本。团队名称是公开品牌，运营主体与联系邮箱见本政策开头。花园数据保存在本机，不设花园服务器、广告、内购或自定义游戏行为埋点。种植模拟可在本地计算，但进入游戏及养护操作需要按发行要求完成 TapTap 登录、实名与可玩时长验证；不能把本版本理解为无需认证的完全离线发行。天气、大致定位和通知仍是可选功能。所有养护参数仅用于游戏，不构成现实植物栽培建议。仅在本政策完整生效、用户主动同意且真实应用配置就绪后，才初始化账号/合规 SDK。

1. Scope and principles

This policy describes the native-compliance Android edition of Zen Farm, developed under the public team name 老干部活动中心 and operated by the publisher identified at the start of this policy, alongside the contact email. Gardens stay on the device; there is no garden server, advertising, in-app purchase or custom gameplay analytics. Simulation runs locally, but entering the game and performing care require TapTap login, real-name and play-time eligibility checks. This edition is not an authentication-free offline distribution. Weather, approximate location and notifications remain optional. Growing parameters are game mechanics, not botanical advice. Account/compliance SDK initialization requires a complete, effective policy, explicit user consent and real application configuration.

二、TapTap 登录、实名与防沉迷

仅在本政策完整生效、用户主动同意且真实应用配置就绪后，才初始化 TapSDK 4.11.0 的核心、登录及合规认证模块。提供方为易玩（上海）网络科技有限公司，隐私政策：https://developer.taptap.cn/docs/sdk/start/agreement/ 。登录只申请 basic_info 范围以获得 openId/unionId；游戏仅使用 openId 关联本次验证，不请求昵称、头像或好友。实名认证和可玩时长检查由 TapTap 服务执行，必要时在 SDK 界面完成实名；我们的花园模型、日志及备份不会自动写入原始姓名、证件号码、手机号或 SDK 登录凭证。为限制未成年人后台交互，会申请年龄段授权，不获取具体出生日期；年龄段和短期验证凭据只用于本进程访问控制，不写入花园备份。SDK 按官方政策处理系统版本、设备型号、CPU/内存、网络类型、Android ID 等必要运行信息。已显式关闭 TapTapEvent 业务统计、自动设备登录事件、自动内购事件及反射 OAID 采集，不发送植物、日记或养护事件给统计接口。官方允许进入游戏的回调才可解锁操作；失败、取消、未知状态和限制结果均不会按成功处理。进程重启或短期会话过期需重新验证，年龄段未知或未成年用户的桌面操作会引导到应用内，由 SDK 管理可玩时段。

2. TapTap login, real-name verification and play limits

TapSDK 4.11.0 core, login and compliance modules initialize only after the policy is effective, the user explicitly agrees and real application configuration is present. The provider is Yiwan (Shanghai) Network Technology Co., Ltd.; policy: https://developer.taptap.cn/docs/sdk/start/agreement/ . Login requests only basic_info for openId/unionId. The game uses openId for verification, without requesting nickname, avatar or friends. TapTap handles real-name and play-time checks through its service and SDK UI. Our garden model, logs and backups do not automatically store raw identity names, document numbers, phone numbers or SDK login credentials. Age-range authorization supports restricted background access; no exact birth date is requested by our controller. Age ranges and short-lived verification proofs are process-local and are not exported with the garden. The SDK processes necessary system version, device model, CPU/memory, network type and Android ID information under its policy. TapTapEvent gameplay analytics, automatic device-login events, automatic purchase events and reflective OAID collection are explicitly disabled; plant, journal and care events are not sent to analytics APIs. Only a genuine SDK allow result unlocks play. Failures, cancellation, unknown results and restrictions never become successful verification. Process restarts or expired proofs require re-verification. Unknown-age and under-18 widget users must open the app so SDK play-time rules apply.

三、本地花园与备份

植物名称、种类、随机绘图种子、生长和健康状态、养护时间、设施、日记、设置及版本信息保存在应用私有目录。桌面组件与主程序共用同一份花园；组件选中的植物标识保存在本机。程序保留一份用于显式恢复的本地副本，存档损坏时不会擅自清空。数据保留至用户清除或卸载；系统云备份与自动设备迁移备份被禁用。导出仅写入用户通过系统文件选择器选定的文档，导入只读取用户选定的文档，不申请广泛存储权限。选定位置可能由第三方文档提供者托管，其处理适用相应服务规则。旧导出和恢复副本可能包含此前选择的天气区域；关闭天气不会修改已经导出的文件。

3. Local garden and backups

Plant names, species, drawing seeds, growth, health, care times, facilities, journal, settings and version data live in app-private storage. Widgets use the same garden; widget selections remain local. One local recovery copy is retained for explicit restoration. Damaged saves are not silently reset. Data remains until you erase it or uninstall. System cloud backup and automatic device-transfer backup are disabled. Exports write only to a document you choose through the system picker; imports read only the document you select. No broad storage permission is requested. A chosen document provider may be a third-party cloud service, subject to its own terms. Old exports and recovery copies can retain a previously selected weather area; disabling weather does not rewrite exported files.

四、可选天气与第三方服务

仅在用户阅读天气说明并主动开启后，应用才向 Open-Meteo 请求天气。手动城市使用预设城市坐标，不需要定位权限。附近天气只保存并发送取整至 0.1 度网格的大致坐标，不发送原始精确位置。请求还包含天气字段、单位和预报时间范围，不含植物名称、花园、日记或备份内容。Open-Meteo（OpenMeteo GmbH）会收到请求坐标、IP 地址等普通连接信息；其政策允许排错日志保留最多 90 天。服务位于第三方基础设施，可能涉及境外处理，请在开启前阅读其政策。政策及条款：https://open-meteo.com/en/terms 。天气数据按 CC BY 4.0 署名：https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ 。小时预报在本地缓存，最多 744 条，缺失时段明确标注为估算。关闭天气会清除当前区域与天气缓存，旧恢复副本及导出文件另行保留。天气失败不会阻止室内玩法，不会偷偷切换成另一个城市。

4. Optional weather and its provider

Weather requests start only after you read the disclosure and explicitly enable the feature. Manual cities use predefined coordinates and need no location permission. Nearby weather saves and sends only coordinates rounded to a 0.1-degree grid, never the original precise location. Requests contain weather fields, units and forecast ranges, not plant names, garden data, journals or backups. Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH) receives the requested coordinates and ordinary connection information such as IP addresses. Its policy permits troubleshooting logs for up to 90 days. Processing on third-party infrastructure may occur outside your country; review its terms before enabling weather: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms . Weather data is attributed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Up to 744 hourly readings are cached locally; missing periods are explicitly estimated. Disabling weather clears the current selection and cache, but not old exports or recovery copies. Weather failures do not block indoor play or silently select another city.

五、权限与后台行为

网络权限用于已同意的账号/合规认证服务及自愿开启的天气请求。大致定位权限仅在主动选择“使用附近的大致位置”时申请；定位仅限前台、单次且有超时，不申请精确位置或后台定位权限。设备的位置服务提供方可能依据系统设置处理位置。组件只使用已保存的天气区域。Android 13 及以上仅在开启提醒时申请通知权限，通知在本地生成。AndroidX WorkManager 使用网络状态、唤醒锁及开机广播恢复已启用的定时任务，系统调度可能延迟任务。用户撤回本政策同意后，本应用停止新发起的 SDK/天气调用，取消受控后台天气工作，退出游戏验证账号并清除本机同意记录；已经发出的请求和服务端必要日志不能被撤回。依赖库声明了前台服务能力，但本版本不启动前台服务、不保持常驻进程。游戏不申请通讯录、电话、短信、麦克风、相机、广告标识符或广泛文件访问权限。

5. Permissions and background behavior

Internet access serves authorized account/compliance verification and optional weather. Approximate location permission is requested only when you choose nearby weather. Requests are foreground-only, one-shot and time-bounded; neither precise nor background location permission is requested. Your device's location provider may process location under its own settings. Widgets use the saved coarse area. Android 13 and later request notification permission only when reminders are enabled; reminders are local. AndroidX WorkManager uses network state, wake locks and boot broadcasts to restore enabled work, subject to system delays. Withdrawing policy consent stops new SDK/weather calls made by this app, cancels controlled weather work, signs out of game verification and clears the local consent record. Already sent requests and necessary provider logs cannot be recalled. A dependency declares foreground-service capability, but this version starts no foreground service or always-running process. No contacts, phone, SMS, microphone, camera, advertising identifier or broad file-access permission is requested.

六、所用组件与共享范围

除上述 TapSDK 外，AndroidX Compose、Activity、Core、Lifecycle 等库用于原生界面与系统适配，WorkManager 用于本地调度，Kotlin 协程和 kotlinx.serialization 用于并发及 JSON，OkHttp 用于已授权的天气 HTTPS 请求。TapSDK 核心会传递依赖 tap-db 等基础组件；本应用关闭可选事件统计，不向其添加自定义游戏事件、玩家画像、广告或购买数据。此配置不代表第三方登录/实名服务不处理其政策列明的必要运行信息。花园数据不上传开发者服务器。操作系统和用户选择的浏览器、位置或文档服务按各自政策提供功能。公开政策链接仅在用户主动打开时交给系统浏览器。公开网页使用 GitHub Pages 静态托管；访问时 GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址，无论是否登录 GitHub。本页面不主动设置 Cookie，不加载统计脚本或远程图片；托管服务的数据处理另适用 GitHub 隐私声明：https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement 。网页访问不会向 GitHub 发送花园、植物或日记数据。

6. Components and sharing

In addition to TapSDK, AndroidX Compose, Activity, Core and Lifecycle provide native UI/platform integration, WorkManager schedules local work, Kotlin coroutines and kotlinx.serialization handle concurrency/JSON, and OkHttp makes authorized weather HTTPS requests. TapSDK core transitively includes components such as tap-db. Optional event analytics are disabled; the app adds no custom gameplay events, player profiles, advertising or purchase data. This does not mean the third-party account/compliance service stops processing the necessary operational information described in its policy. Gardens are not uploaded to a developer server. Your operating system, browser, location and document providers operate under their respective policies. Policy URLs open in the system browser only when you choose them. The public page uses GitHub Pages static hosting. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. This page does not itself set cookies or load analytics scripts or remote images. Hosting data processing is covered separately by GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement . Visiting the page does not send garden, plant or journal data to GitHub.

七、管理、导出与删除

可关闭天气/提醒，或在系统设置中撤回定位/通知权限；设置中的“退出验证账号”清除本游戏的 TapTap 登录缓存，不会注销或退出 TapTap 客户端。“撤回隐私同意”会退出本游戏验证并停止后续受控调用，重新游玩需再次同意有效政策并认证。登录失败或拒绝政策时仍可导出、删除已有花园，不会为了认证而重置数据。清除全部本地数据删除花园、日记、养护设置、组件选择和恢复副本，并退出验证、清除同意记录；系统通知历史和第三方依法保留的日志不由本地操作删除。用户自选导出文件、云文档副本需自行管理。一台设备保留一份本地花园，切换 TapTap 账号不会自动隔离、清空或云同步花园；需要分离时请先导出再清除。TapTap 账号/实名数据及 Open-Meteo 日志的权利请求，应通过相应服务的官方渠道处理。

7. Managing, exporting and deleting data

Disable weather/reminders or revoke system location/notification permissions. Sign out of verification clears this game's cached TapTap login, not the TapTap client account. Withdrawing consent signs out of game verification and stops subsequent controlled calls; playing again requires consent to an effective policy and new verification. Existing gardens can still be exported or deleted if login fails or consent is declined, and verification never silently resets them. Erase all local data removes the garden, journal, care settings, widget selections and recovery copy, signs out and clears consent. System notification history and provider logs lawfully retained are not deleted by local erasure. Exported documents and cloud-provider copies remain under your control. This device has one local garden: switching TapTap accounts does not isolate, erase or cloud-sync it. Export and erase explicitly if separation is needed. Requests concerning TapTap account/real-name information or Open-Meteo logs should use those providers' official channels.
