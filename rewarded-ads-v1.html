一、适用范围与基本原则

本说明适用于老干部活动中心制作、由本政策载明的运营主体提供的《佛系种地》Android 可选激励广告版本（versionCode 6）。团队名称是公开品牌，运营主体与联系邮箱见本政策开头。花园、种植进度、日记与模拟天气在本机处理，不设花园服务器、内购、联机或自定义游戏行为埋点。原有种子、三种花盆和养护功能仍免费；近期已验证的成年用户可以主动选择观看广告，仅用于解锁额外外观，不改变成长规则。天气不对应真实城市或预报，不读取定位、系统天气应用或天气服务。进入游戏仍需 TapTap 登录、实名与可玩时长验证；选择广告也会联网，不能将本地玩法理解为整个应用离线。仅在本政策完整生效、用户主动同意且真实配置就绪后初始化账号/合规 SDK；广告 SDK 还需要本次明确的广告选择，未配置广告时不会初始化。旧 versionCode 5 不含广告，适用 https://onecentclawd.github.io/zen-farm-privacy/local-weather-v1.html ；versionCode 3/4 适用 https://onecentclawd.github.io/zen-farm-privacy/ 。本政策不追溯改变旧安装包的数据行为。

1. Scope and principles

This policy covers the optional rewarded-ad Android edition of Zen Farm, versionCode 6, developed under the public team name 老干部活动中心 and operated by the publisher identified above. Gardens, growth, journals and simulated weather remain local, without a garden server, in-app purchase, multiplayer or custom gameplay analytics. Original seeds, three pot styles and care stay free. Recently verified adults may actively choose an ad to unlock an additional cosmetic, without changing growth rules. Weather is simulated, not a real forecast, and reads no location, other weather app or weather service. TapTap login, real-name and play-time verification still require network access; optional ads also use the network. Local gameplay is not a promise that the entire app works offline. Account/compliance SDK initialization requires an effective policy, explicit consent and real configuration. Advertising additionally requires an explicit choice for that ad attempt and never initializes when configuration is absent. The ad-free versionCode 5 uses https://onecentclawd.github.io/zen-farm-privacy/local-weather-v1.html ; versionCodes 3 and 4 use https://onecentclawd.github.io/zen-farm-privacy/ . This policy does not retroactively change older APK behavior.

2. TapTap login, real-name verification and play limits

TapSDK 4.11.0 core, login and compliance modules initialize only after the policy is effective, the user explicitly agrees and real application configuration is present. The provider is Yiwan (Shanghai) Network Technology Co., Ltd.; policy: https://developer.taptap.cn/docs/sdk/start/agreement/ . Login requests only basic_info for openId/unionId. The game uses openId for verification, without requesting nickname, avatar or friends. TapTap handles real-name and play-time checks through its service and SDK UI. Our garden model, logs and backups do not automatically store raw identity names, document numbers, phone numbers or SDK login credentials. Age-range authorization supports restricted background access; no exact birth date is requested by our controller. Age ranges and short-lived verification proofs are process-local and are not exported with the garden. The SDK processes necessary system version, device model, CPU/memory, network type and Android ID information under its policy. TapTapEvent gameplay analytics, automatic device-login events, automatic purchase events and reflective OAID collection are explicitly disabled; plant, journal and care events are not sent to analytics APIs. Only a genuine SDK allow result unlocks play. Failures, cancellation, unknown results and restrictions never become successful verification. Process restarts or expired proofs require re-verification. Unknown-age and under-18 widget users must open the app so SDK play-time rules apply.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

We integrate the non-mediation TapADN SDK 5.3.2.0 from Shanghai ADN Digital Technology Co., Ltd. and its affiliates; policy: https://ssp.dirichlet.cn/docs/agreement/ . Initialization and an ad request require consent to an effective policy, real media/placement configuration, a current adult session established by genuine TapTap compliance results, and an explicit Agree and watch ad action. Opening settings, ordinary gardening, reminders and widgets do not request ads. Minors, unknown ages and expired/revoked sessions cannot request or show new ads. For delivery, attribution, statistics and anti-fraud, the SDK processes necessary device brand/model, system version, display information, language/time zone, CPU/storage, app package/version/foreground state, network runtime information and IP address, ad impressions/clicks/conversions, anti-fraud runtime identifiers, performance and crash information, subject to its actual configuration and official policy. We explicitly disable optional location, phone-state/serial, Wi-Fi details, MAC/BSSID/SSID, external-storage, OAID, Android ID, installed-app-list and sensor switches, shake interactions and personalized recommendations. We provide no manual location, device identifier, user attributes or TapTap account identifier to advertising APIs. This can reduce fill or revenue and does not mean necessary operational or anti-fraud data processing stops. The merged manifest removes broad package-query, external-app installation and vendor OAID permissions, while retaining the SDK's normal secure-key-service access for anti-fraud. We do not request fingerprint, face or other biometric data. External destinations chosen by an ad click apply their own policies; the game does not consent to third-party installation on your behalf. Declining, cancelling, failing to load or receiving no ad never blocks free gardening. Only a genuine reward-verification result tied to the current eligible adult attempt authorizes the cosmetic. Video completion, clicks or closing alone do not grant it; duplicate or stale callbacks do not grant again. Starlight is a local cosmetic without cash value, not a payment or investment return. Loading can be cancelled by leaving the panel. Policy withdrawal or a changed verification session stops further controlled ad calls, but cannot recall requests already sent or erase provider logs lawfully retained.

四、本地花园与备份

植物名称、种类、随机绘图种子、生长和健康状态、养护时间、设施、日记、设置、本地天气模拟种子及版本信息保存在应用私有目录。桌面组件与主程序共用同一份花园；组件选中的植物标识保存在本机。程序保留一份用于显式恢复的本地副本，存档损坏时不会擅自清空。数据保留至用户清除或卸载；系统云备份与自动设备迁移备份被禁用。导出仅写入用户通过系统文件选择器选定的文档，导入只读取用户选定的文档，不申请广泛存储权限。选定位置可能由第三方文档提供者托管，其处理适用相应服务规则。载入、导入或恢复旧天气存档时，会移除正在使用的城市、坐标与联网天气缓存，并将原已开启的天气转换为本地模拟，保留植物和日记。升级前的外部导出或历史恢复副本可能仍含旧区域，需自行管理；本版本不会联网使用这些区域。

4. Local garden and backups

Plant names, species, drawing seeds, growth, health, care times, facilities, journals, settings, the local weather simulation seed and version data live in app-private storage. Widgets use the same garden; widget selections remain local. One recovery copy is retained for explicit restoration. Damaged saves are not silently reset. Data remains until you erase it or uninstall. System cloud backup and automatic device-transfer backup are disabled. Exports write only to a document you choose through the system picker; imports read only the selected document. No broad storage permission is requested. A selected document provider may be a third-party cloud service with its own terms. Loading, importing or restoring an older weather save removes the active city, coordinates and online weather cache and converts previously enabled weather to local simulation, preserving plants and journals. Earlier external exports or historical recovery copies may still contain an old area and remain under your control; this version does not use those areas for network requests.

五、本地随机模拟天气

默认保留室内养护规则，可自愿开启本地随机天气。模拟使用花园的本地种子与固定游戏时钟生成温度、湿度、雨雪、日照和风，不读取真实位置或其他应用的数据，也不连接天气服务器。相同种子与小时生成相同结果，刷新、重启和支持该设置的备份恢复不会重新抽取；开启状态及种子随花园备份保存，不用于识别设备或建立用户画像。模拟结果在界面和组件中明确标为模拟，不是现实天气或预报。单次离线补算最多处理 31 天逐小时模拟，超出部分按温和气候估算，仍沿用天气模式成长规则。旧客户端可能忽略新增模拟设置并继续室内模式；植物、日记等兼容数据仍可导入。

5. Local random simulated weather

Indoor care remains the default; local random weather is optional. A garden-local seed and fixed game-clock cycle generate temperature, humidity, rain or snow, sunlight and wind without reading real location or another app's data and without contacting weather servers. The same seed and hour produce the same result; refreshing, restarting or restoring a backup in a supporting client does not reroll it. The enabled state and seed are saved with the garden, not used to identify a device or profile a user. Screens and widgets explicitly label these results as simulated, not real weather or forecasts. One offline catch-up replays at most 31 days of hourly simulation; longer gaps use mild-climate estimates while retaining weather-mode growth rules. Older clients may ignore the added simulation setting and continue indoors; compatible plant and journal data can still be imported.

六、权限与后台行为

网络权限用于已同意的 TapTap 账号/合规认证，以及用户主动选择的激励广告及其素材、必要统计与反作弊通信，不用于天气或花园云同步。本版本不申请大致、精确或后台定位权限。Android 13 及以上仅在开启提醒时申请通知权限，提醒内容在本地生成。AndroidX WorkManager 使用网络状态、唤醒锁及开机广播等基础能力调度本地任务；这些任务不请求广告或天气，系统省电策略可能延迟执行。撤回本政策同意后停止后续受控 SDK 调用、取消受控后台任务、退出游戏验证并清除同意记录；已经发出的请求和服务端必要日志不能被撤回。广告加载时退到后台会取消该次加载；SDK 自有前台广告页面播放时，仍需当前成年验证有效，关闭后返回花园，不在桌面组件、通知或游戏退出后的后台展示广告。应用不启动常驻前台服务，也不申请通讯录、电话、短信、麦克风、相机、生物识别、广告标识符或广泛文件访问权限。

6. Permissions and background behavior

Internet access serves authorized TapTap account/compliance verification and actively chosen rewarded ads, creatives, necessary ad statistics and anti-fraud communication, not weather or garden cloud synchronization. No approximate, precise or background location permission is requested. Android 13 and later request notification permission only for optional local reminders. AndroidX WorkManager uses network-state access, wake locks and boot broadcasts to schedule local jobs; these jobs do not request ads or weather and may be delayed by battery policies. Policy withdrawal stops further controlled SDK calls, cancels controlled jobs, signs out of game verification and clears consent. Requests already sent and necessary provider logs cannot be recalled. Backgrounding during ad loading cancels that attempt. The SDK's own foreground advertising page still requires the current adult proof; closing returns to the garden. Ads do not appear in widgets, notifications or after the game is exited into the background. The app starts no persistent foreground service and requests no contacts, phone, SMS, microphone, camera, biometric, advertising-identifier or broad file-access permission.

七、所用组件与共享范围

第三方服务包括上述 TapSDK 4.11.0 账号/合规模块与独立的 TapADN 5.3.2.0 广告模块，本版本未接入其它广告联盟聚合。AndroidX Compose、Activity、Core、Lifecycle 用于原生界面与系统适配，WorkManager 用于本地调度，Kotlin 协程和 kotlinx.serialization 用于并发及 JSON，OkHttp 4.12.0 为广告 SDK 提供网络传输。本应用没有恢复天气 HTTP 客户端。TapSDK 核心包含 tap-db 等组件，本应用继续关闭其可选事件统计，不把植物、日记、养护或购买事件发送给账号服务；选择广告后的展示与归因等信息由广告 SDK 按第三节处理，不能将二者混同。花园与外观解锁数据不上传开发者服务器，不通过广告接口发送 TapTap 登录标识、植物名称或日记。操作系统、用户选择的浏览器与文档服务适用各自政策。公开政策链接仅在用户主动打开时交给系统浏览器。网页使用 GitHub Pages 静态托管，GitHub 出于安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址，无论是否登录。本页面不主动设置 Cookie，不加载统计脚本或远程图片；托管服务适用 https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement 。访问政策页不会发送花园数据。

八、管理、导出与删除

可关闭模拟天气或提醒，并在系统设置中撤回通知权限；关闭模拟天气后使用室内规则，不会请求真实天气。设置中的“退出验证账号”清除本游戏的 TapTap 登录缓存，不会注销或退出 TapTap 客户端。“撤回隐私同意”会退出本游戏验证并停止后续受控调用，重新游玩需再次同意有效政策并认证。登录失败或拒绝政策时仍可导出、删除已有花园，不会为了认证而重置数据。清除全部本地数据删除花园、日记、养护设置、组件选择和恢复副本，并退出验证、清除同意记录；系统通知历史和第三方依法保留的日志不由本地操作删除。用户自选导出文件、云文档副本及旧版本可能产生的第三方记录需自行管理。一台设备保留一份本地花园，切换 TapTap 账号不会自动隔离、清空或云同步花园；需要分离时请先导出再清除。TapTap 账号/实名数据等第三方数据的权利请求，应通过相应服务的官方渠道处理。

8. Managing, exporting and deleting data

Disable simulated weather or reminders, or revoke notification permission in system settings. Turning simulation off uses indoor rules without requesting real weather. Sign out of verification clears this game's cached TapTap login, not the TapTap client account. Withdrawing consent signs out of game verification and stops subsequent controlled calls; playing again requires consent to an effective policy and new verification. Existing gardens can still be exported or deleted if login fails or consent is declined, and verification never silently resets them. Erase all local data removes the garden, journal, care settings, widget selections and recovery copy, signs out and clears consent. System notification history and provider logs lawfully retained are not deleted by local erasure. Exported documents, cloud-provider copies and third-party records from earlier editions remain separately managed. This device has one local garden: switching TapTap accounts does not isolate, erase or cloud-sync it. Export and erase explicitly if separation is needed. Rights requests about third-party data such as TapTap account or real-name information should use the provider's official channels.
